Who You Contract With & Who Is Liable

The liability map — plain English · Effective 22 September 2026
Short version: the airline is responsible for flying you. eZay is responsible for booking accurately, showing you the right price and rules, issuing your ticket through the right route and passing on what the airline tells us. Duffel and Faremine are the ticketing intermediaries behind the scenes and Stripe is the payment processor; you do not have a contract with any of them.

2. What eZay is liable for

  • Loss caused by our own negligence or breach of these terms — for example, ticketing a different date from the one you confirmed.
  • Our liability is limited to the total price you paid us for the affected booking, except for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, fraud, or anything else that cannot be limited by law.

3. What eZay is not liable for

  • The performance of the flight: delays, cancellations, schedule changes, overbooking, baggage, or injury on board — these are the airline's responsibility.
  • Refusal of travel or entry due to passport, visa, health or immigration issues.
  • Errors in information you gave us (names, dates, contact details).
  • Airline insolvency (see Refunds & Cancellations, section 5).
  • Anything you book with a third party through a link on our site.
  • Events outside anyone's control (weather, strikes, airspace closures, pandemics).

5. Financial protection statement

eZay is not an ATOL holder and is not a member of ABTA. Flight-only tickets issued immediately on payment fall outside the ATOL scheme. Where a booking is fulfilled through our ATOL-holding consolidator, Faremine Ltd, the ATOL Certificate you receive states what is protected. We strongly recommend travel insurance that covers scheduled airline failure for every booking.
