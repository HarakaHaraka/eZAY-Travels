How eZay works, how we handle your money, and the basis on which we deal with you · Effective 22 September 2026
In one paragraph: eZay Travels is a UK online travel agent. When you book a flight with us we arrange it on behalf of the airline or ticketing principal; your contract of carriage is with that airline. Your ticket is issued through one of two ticketing routes — Duffel (IATA-accredited platform) or Faremine (IATA/ATOL consolidator) — and your payment is taken by card through Stripe. We show you one all-in price before you pay. We are not a tour operator, we do not sell packages, and we hold no client money in trust.

  • The price you see at the point of booking is the total price including fare, taxes, airline charges and eZay's service margin. No mandatory charge is added at checkout.

4. Payments handling

  1. How you pay. All payments are taken in GBP by debit or credit card through Stripe Payments UK Ltd, our payment processor. Card details are entered on Stripe's secure form; we never see or store your full card number. We may ask for 3-D Secure authentication from your bank.
  2. When money moves. Your card is charged when you press Pay. Stripe holds the funds and settles them to eZay's business bank account. From that account we pay the ticketing route: Duffel is settled automatically against the ticket; Faremine is prepaid by bank transfer before the ticket is issued.
  3. Failed or declined payments. If Stripe or your bank declines the payment, no booking is made and nothing is charged. If a fare is withdrawn between payment and issue, we refund you in full to the same card, normally within 5 working days.
  4. Client money. We do not hold client money in trust. Your payment becomes eZay's receipt on which we settle the supplier, and the airline's obligations are secured by the issued ticket, not by any fund we hold.
  5. Refunds. Every refund goes back to the original card through Stripe; we cannot refund to a different card, by bank transfer or in cash. Timing depends on the ticketing route — see Refunds & Cancellations.
  6. Chargebacks. Please contact us before raising a chargeback with your bank. A chargeback on a validly issued ticket will be contested with the airline's ticketing records.
  7. Bookings by enquiry. Where a fare is not bookable online (group, unaccompanied minor, complex routing), we quote by email or WhatsApp and send you a secure Stripe payment link. We do not take card details by phone, email or message.

5. How we work (company protocol)

  • Hours. Online booking is available 24 hours a day. Our service desk answers email, WhatsApp and phone between 08:00 and 22:00 UK time, seven days a week; urgent day-of-travel messages received outside those hours are handled first thing the next morning.
  • Response times. We acknowledge every message within one working day and give a full answer within three working days, except where we are waiting on an airline, in which case we tell you and update you every seven days.
  • Languages. We serve you in English and, on request, in other languages spoken by our team.
  • One record. Every booking, change, refund and complaint is logged against your booking reference, so any member of the team can pick it up.
  • No pressure selling. We do not add pre-ticked extras, countdown timers or "only 1 seat left" claims that are not the airline's own live data.
