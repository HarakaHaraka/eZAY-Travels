How we use your personal data · Effective 22 September 2026

1. Controller

eZAY Travels and Tours Ltd (company no. 17394853), 181 Barcombe Avenue, Lambeth, London SW2 3BH, manager@ezaytravels.co.uk. We are registered with the Information Commissioner's Office.

2. What we collect

  • Passenger details: name, date of birth, gender as required by the airline, nationality, passport number/expiry where required for the route, contact details.
  • Booking details: itinerary, fare, extras, special requests (which may include health-related information such as assistance needs or special meals — only what you choose to give us).
  • Payment: handled by Stripe; we receive a confirmation and the last four digits only. We never hold your full card number.
  • Website use: cookies and analytics (see cookie banner), search history on our site, correspondence with us by email, phone or WhatsApp.

4. Who we share it with

The airline operating your flight; Duffel Technology Ltd or Faremine Ltd as ticketing intermediaries; Stripe Payments UK Ltd for payment and fraud screening; government and border authorities where required by law; our IT hosting providers (UK/EU or under UK adequacy/standard contractual clauses); WhatsApp/Meta where you choose to message us on WhatsApp; our insurers and professional advisers where a claim or dispute arises. Airlines and destination authorities may be outside the UK — transfers necessary to perform your booking are made under UK GDPR Article 49(1)(b).

5. Retention

Booking and correspondence records: 6 years after travel (tax and limitation periods). Passport images used for verification: deleted once verified. Marketing preferences: until you opt out. Website analytics: 26 months.

6. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent. Email manager@ezaytravels.co.uk; we respond within one month. You may complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk.

7. Security

Data is stored on access-controlled systems with multi-factor authentication; card data never touches our servers. We will notify you and the ICO of any breach that affects your rights, as the law requires.

8. Photos and user content

If you send us photos or tips for our area guides you confirm you own them and consent to us publishing them with credit. Do not send images that identify other people unless they have agreed.
